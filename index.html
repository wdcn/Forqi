01 About Us

Public institutions deserve the same quality of technology as the best-run private companies — delivered with the transparency, security, and accountability that public work demands.

Forqi LLC is a technology and AI consultancy dedicated to government and the public sector. We work alongside agency teams as partners, not vendors — honest advice about what AI can and can't do, solutions sized to real budgets, and knowledge transfer so your staff can own what we build together.

From first assessment to deployed solution, our commitment is the same: practical technology that improves how government serves people.

03 Our Approach

How we work

Every engagement follows a clear, accountable path — no black boxes, no surprises.

Listen & Assess

We start with your mission, not our tools. We learn your processes, constraints, and goals, and assess where technology can make a measurable difference.

STEP 02

Design & Prove

We design a right-sized solution and prove it with a small pilot before you commit — so decisions are based on evidence, not promises.

STEP 03

Build & Deliver

We build with security, accessibility, and compliance in from day one, delivering in short increments you can see and steer.

STEP 04

Transfer & Support

We train your team, document everything, and hand over ownership — staying available for support without locking you in.
