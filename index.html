01 Qllect® Platform

Human intelligence, on demand

Qllect® is Forqi's crowd-sourcing platform. Define what you need, and Qllect recruits the right contributors, collects the data or research, verifies quality and delivers results ready to use.

For AI teams, product & UX researchers, and anyone who needs real human input at scale

Capabilities
Q.01

Data Generation

Mobile and web collection of speech, images, video, text and sensor data to your spec for languages, demographics, devices and scenes.

Q.02

User Research

Surveys, unmoderated usability tests, moderated interviews, diary studies and concept tests with targeted participants.

Q.03

Annotation & RLHF

Labeling, transcription, preference ranking, red-teaming and model evaluation by trained and expert contributors.

Q.04

Targeted Recruiting

Screeners and qualification tests to reach the right people by language, region, device, profession or expertise.

Q.05

Quality Engine

Gold tasks, consensus scoring, reviewer tiers and fraud detection, with QA metrics reported on every batch.

Q.06

Consent & Payouts

E-consent, privacy controls and contributor payments handled in-platform, with provenance records that ship with your data.

05 About Forqi

Good AI starts with trustworthy data and ends with systems people can rely on. Forqi works at both ends, supplying the data models learn from and delivering AI that creates real value.

Forqi LLC is a U.S.-based technology and AI company with three offerings. Qllect®, our crowd-sourcing platform, generates data and runs user research on demand. Our Dataset Services license curated, previewable training data. Our AI Consulting practice works alongside businesses of every size and public-sector teams as a partner rather than a vendor.

They reinforce each other. Qllect produces the data behind our catalog and custom projects. Building data for model makers keeps our consulting grounded in how AI really works, and enterprise and public-sector delivery sets our bar for security, transparency and accountability.

Data-Grounded
